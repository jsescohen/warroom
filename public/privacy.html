Privacy policy

Warroom (beta) · last updated 7 October 2026

Warroom is a small browser strategy game run by one independent developer for a closed group of beta testers. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in account: when you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your email address, your name on that service and your profile picture. Sign-in is handled by Supabase; the game never sees your password.
  • Your username, which you choose and which other players can see.
  • Your saved games, if you save while signed in.
  • Basic account dates: when you joined and when you last played.
  • Messages to AI leaders: what you write to in-game leaders is sent to an AI service (currently Groq) to generate their replies. Do not write personal information there.

How it is used

Only to run the game: to let you in (the admin approves beta testers), to keep your saves available on any device, and to show your username. Your email and real name are visible only to the game's admin, never to other players.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run the game: Supabase (sign-in and database), Render (hosting) and the AI provider for leader replies.

Cookies and local storage

The game stores your sign-in session and your settings in your browser, and saves made without signing in stay only in your browser. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your saves in the game at any time. To delete your whole account, contact the developer at the support address shown on the sign-in consent screen, and it will be removed.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
